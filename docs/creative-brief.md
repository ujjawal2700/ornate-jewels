# The Ornate Edit

A customer storefront for a Kota boutique with personal curation by Piyusha. Balance statement heritage jewellery with contemporary diamond styling. The demo serves two journeys: purchase at sample prices; save a shortlist and request personal guidance.

Palette: Porcelain #fbf9f5, Sand #e9dfd0, Espresso #33281f, Antique gold #a2814c, Forest #263f35, Pale sage #eef0e9.
Typography: Cormorant Garamond for expressive display type and Manrope for readable controls.
Composition: a campaign portrait with quiet space for a large, left-aligned headline; a compact category gallery; a focused product edit; alternating portrait-led stories and shopping. Thin framing details take their cue from jewellery settings. Avoid ornamental cards and gratuitous badges.

Original proposal review: retained the approved warm palette, but made the campaign image and actual Ornate jewellery the signature rather than a collection of decorative luxury motifs. Product grids remain useful and calm. The one dramatic animation belongs to the opening campaign; other motion responds to action.

Assets sourced from Ornate's public website for this private client-pitch concept, not licensed for an unrelated public store. Names, prices, specifications, availability and policies are sample demonstration content, not verified commercial offers. No fabricated customer testimonials or ratings.
